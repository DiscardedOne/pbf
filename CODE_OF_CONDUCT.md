# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Project Blueprint Framework (PBF) welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds, experience levels and perspectives. We are committed to making project discussions and contributions respectful, constructive and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate and constructive.
- Assume good faith while remaining open to correction.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence and clear reasoning.
- Accept that maintainers may decline changes that conflict with project scope, architecture or release policy.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information, credentials, private project material or confidential reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats or personal attacks.
- Deliberate disruption, repeated bad-faith conduct or abusive communication.
- Publishing private information or confidential security details without authorization.
- Impersonation, deceptive attribution or knowingly misrepresenting project evidence.

## Scope

This policy applies in PBF project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, code review and project-related public communications when someone is representing or participating in the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For content hosted on GitHub, use GitHub's report-content or abuse-reporting controls and identify the PBF repository. For a matter that requires direct maintainer attention, use the private contact method displayed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive personal information or an unpatched vulnerability in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports as reasonably practicable, protect reporter confidentiality where possible, and determine an appropriate response. Responses may include editing or removing content, declining or reverting contributions, issuing a warning, or restricting participation. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for conduct that creates a safety or project-integrity risk.

This policy is intended to support a respectful project space; it does not promise a particular response time or replace GitHub's platform policies.
